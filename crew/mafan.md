## Ability
**Zoltan** (innate): +1 Reactor Core, but reduces the mech's HP to 1 and grants a Shield every turn. The per-turn shield cannot be fully disrupted (unlike pilot-granted starting shields), making Mafan the preferred pilot for self-freezing Ice Mech builds. **Blacklisted from HP skills** (+2 HP / Skilled / Masochist) since patch 1.0.17.
