# Into the Breach -- Controls

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02

PC keyboard/mouse and controller controls. Keyboard hotkeys are fully remappable in-game; a separate in-game gamepad settings menu also exists.

## Keyboard & Mouse (PC default)

Confirmed bindings: **number keys** fire weapons; **Spacebar = End Turn** (default); hold **Alt** = attack-order / effects overlay; hold **Ctrl** = unit info; **` (tilde)** = dev console (launch with `-console` if it doesn't open). A 2018 GameFAQs FAQ lists **A/S/D = select Mech 1/2/3** and **F/G/H = deployed units**. [Confirmed: 2 sources]

> **Unverified default keys:** exact defaults for Undo Move, Reset Turn, zoom, and mech-cycle are NOT corroborated in documentation (only the A/S/D mech-select scheme and Spacebar/Alt/Ctrl/tilde are). The P1 brief's "Tab/Q/E cycle" could not be confirmed. See `limitations.md`.

## Controller

Full native support (added Nov 2, 2018); the UI was designed for gamepads. Plug in and press any button to switch modes. XInput and PlayStation prompts both selectable; haptics can be disabled. Steam Deck has an official layout (left stick = cursor). The exact per-button map could not be confirmed from documentation. [Confirmed: 2 sources]

## Notable mechanics

- **Undo Move:** unlimited per turn, MOVEMENT ONLY — you cannot move after firing, and firing cannot be undone. Undo can be blocked if a later unit's action affected the vacated tile. [Confirmed: 2 sources]
- **Reset Turn:** once per battle by default; rewinds all units to the start of YOUR turn and lets all act again. "Battle" = one map/mission section (not per island/run). You cannot reset after the enemy turn resolves. Isaac Jones's Temporal Reset grants +1 reset (total 2); Adam's Chosen One triggers on reset (Shield + 2 Move that turn). [Confirmed: 2 sources]
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-003: Isaac Jones doubles Reset Turn uses to 2 per battle. DEP-012: Adam's Chosen One triggers on Reset Turn use, granting Shield + 2 Move that turn.

## Recommended remaps

Players commonly move **Undo Move** away from the End Turn button (frequent misclick) — to Z or Ctrl+Z — and bind End Turn to Spacebar/Backspace. Keyboard hotkeys and gamepad bindings are both fully remappable in-game. [Confirmed: 2 sources]
