# Into the Breach -- Weapons

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02

Mech weapons: squad-default loadouts, the universal time-pod/vendor pool, and damage/push specs. Upgrade costs are in Reactor Cores (●). All weapons have friendly fire ON unless noted. Reactor upgrade mechanics live in `items/upgrades.md`; build synergies in `items/builds.md`.

## Overview

Each mech carries one or two weapon/equipment slots. Squad-default weapons are often unique to that squad; secondary slots can be filled with weapons from Time Pods or corporate vendors (vendor cost: **2 Reputation** per weapon; the 2022 Advanced Edition raised it back from the v1.2 temporary reduction to 1 rep — AE patch notes are authoritative). Some "weapons" are passives or deployables rather than direct attacks. [Confirmed: 2 sources]

## Universal weapon pool (Time Pod / vendor)

NOT squad-exclusive; any mech can equip them (with a power penalty if off-class). Documented examples: Vortex Fist (2 dmg + 2 self, push all adjacent), Rocket Fist, Sidewinder Fist, Titanite Blade (2 dmg, push 3), Rock Launcher, Mercury Fist (4 dmg, push adjacent), Prime Spear, Heavy Rocket (3 dmg), Astra Bombs, Hermes Engines, Defensive Shrapnel, Confuse Shot (flip attack direction), Fire Beam, Frost Beam, Smoke Pellets, Targeted Strike, Wind Torrent, Teleporter, The Big One; plus deployable **support items**: Light Tank, Shield-Tank, A.C.I.D. Tank, Pull-Tank, Shield Projector, smoke generators. Mainstream guides under-document this pool because pod/store contents are randomized. [Confirmed: 2 sources]

## Mech base-stat notes

Thruster Mech and Drill Mech (3 HP / 4 Move / Flying) are the best flying mechs; Judo Mech (3 HP / 4 Move / Armored) is the best armored mech; Pitcher Mech (4 HP) has the highest base HP of any mech. [Single source -- verify · class: wiki]
