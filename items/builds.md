## Squad tiering (community consensus)

- **Strongest base (Normal/Hard):** Frozen Titans (Ice Mech freeze-control generates huge tempo — CelJaded ranks it top tier), Rusting Hulks (smoke + Storm Generator). The AE **Mist Eaters** are widely called near "easy mode" (two mechs each neutralize 2+ Vek actions per move). [Confirmed: 3 sources]
- **Weakest/hardest:** Blitzkrieg (low starting movement, weak Hook Mech, heavily upgrade-dependent — hardest pre-patch on Hard), Zenith Guard (scales poorly), Heat Sinkers (awkward prime). [Confirmed: 2 sources]
- **Disagreement:** some players rank Bombermechs/Arachnophiles among the EASIEST AE squads; others rate Arachnophiles "bad" for low initial damage. Cataclysm = high burst / instant-kill via crack-into-chasm (TheGamer's AE MVP). [Confirmed: 2 sources]

## Custom Squad — strongest compositions

- **3× Ice Mech + Bethany Jones or Mafan** (a shielded pilot negates the Cryo-Launcher's self-freeze) — community calls it "practically unbeatable"; clears Hard easily and completes Flight + Mech Specialist achievements at once. Mafan is preferred (shield can't be fully disrupted). [Confirmed: 3 sources]
- **3 Prime:** Laser + Combat (Dash Punch, Gana) + Judo (tank) — high repositioning, easy laser line-ups. [Single source]
- **Smoke build:** Jet + Rocket + Smog — cover the map in smoke, heal. [Confirmed: 2 sources]
- **Triple Ice + Archimedes/Silica** — de-ice and fire in the same turn. [Confirmed: 2 sources]

## Difficulty scaling notes

The final battle does NOT scale to your cores/upgrades — only difficulty and run length matter. On Unfair, island-4 regular missions each spawn a Vek Leader, so favor squads with reliable single-target burst or strong displacement (Frozen Titans, Mist Eaters, Cataclysm) over low-damage squads (Arachnophiles, Heat Sinkers). [Confirmed: 2 sources]
