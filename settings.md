## Difficulty

Easy, Normal, Hard, Unfair (Unfair added in Advanced Edition). What changes:
- **Easy** — no alpha Vek outside 3-objective missions on islands 1–2, no Bot Leader, fewer Vek, ≤1 rare Vek type/island, 500 civilians/region (perfect island 2,500; 4-island run 10,000 score).
- **Normal** — alphas from island 2, Bot Leader appears, 1,000 civ/region (perfect run 20,000).
- **Hard** — more Vek, higher alpha frequency, 2,000 civ/region; 1 Vek Leader present in the final battle.
- **Unfair** — even more Vek, a Vek Leader in every island-4 regular mission (replacing one Boss-variant enemy), two bosses on the island-4 HQ and in each Volcanic Hive phase on 3/4-island runs (perfect run 40,000).

Difficulty is effectively locked per timeline; whether it can be changed mid-run is undocumented (likely locked). [Confirmed: 2 sources]

## Gameplay & autosave

- **Autosave:** the game autosaves after every turn (roguelike design — no in-game save-scumming; only external profile-folder copying circumvents it). [Confirmed: 2 sources]
- **Advanced Content toggles:** all Advanced Edition content (enemies, pilot skills, missions, weapons) can be individually toggled off to play the base experience; balance changes are NOT reversible. [Confirmed: 2 sources]

## Recommended settings for new players

Choose difficulty deliberately (it is effectively locked per timeline); enable colorblind mode if Alpha-icon readability matters; remap Undo Move off the End-Turn button to avoid misclicks (see `controls.md`). [Confirmed: 2 sources]
