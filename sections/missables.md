# Sections -- Missables

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02

Aggregated catalog of every missable pickup in the game — the cross-cutting "what am I about to lose?" reference. Into the Breach's missables are per-mission pickups rather than per-zone collectibles (procedural layouts mean there is no fixed collectible map).

## Time Pods

Optional per-mission pickups (islands 1–2 = 1 pod each; islands 3–4 = 2 pods each). Each always yields a reward (reactor core, pilot, grid power, or weapon). **Missable:** a pod is destroyed if a Vek kills it before you pick it up, or if you leave the mission without collecting it. [Confirmed: 2 sources]

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` SEQ-004: Vek ending on the pod tile permanently destroys it; 1 guaranteed reactor core and any bonus reward are lost.

## Strange Pods (secret-pilot unlock)

A Strange Pod has a ~15% chance of replacing a Time Pod on islands 2/3/4; only one may spawn per timeline. Recovering it (Strange Object → Strange Pod → protect/recover) unlocks one secret FTL-crossover pilot (Kazaaakpleth / Mafan / Ariadne). **Missable & fully random** — there is no guaranteed spawn. The "Distant Friends" achievement requires the legit unlock (cheat-code unlocks do not count). See `crew/index.md`. [Confirmed: 2 sources]
_source: deep-research handoff 2026-06-02 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: easter-egg · spoiler: late-game · missable: yes · ach: distant_friends_
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` SEQ-001: Strange Pod recovery is the only way to unlock secret FTL pilots; one per timeline, only on islands 2/3/4, never on Archive (island 1).

## Perfect Island bonus

Completing every objective on an island (including all pods) grants the Perfect Island reward (choice of pilot / weapon / grid / core). **Missable:** failing any objective forfeits it for that island. Feeds the Perfect Island, The Defenders, Untouchable, and Perfect Strategy achievements. [Confirmed: 2 sources]
_source: deep-research handoff 2026-06-02 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression · missable: yes_
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` SEQ-002: Failing any single mission objective or losing any pod forfeits the Perfect Island reward with no recovery before island advance.

> Note: most "missable" pressure in Into the Breach is soft — a new run regenerates all pods and pilots. The genuinely run-unique loss is the Strange Pod (one per timeline) and per-mission pods destroyed before pickup.
