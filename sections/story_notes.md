## The Renfield Bomb & the Vek origin (story)

The Renfield Bomb (named for its developer "Dr. Renfield") is the final-battle superweapon; its detonation is visible from space and ends the Vek threat in that timeline. The Vek originate from the Volcanic Hive island, which causes the flooding referenced in Archive's Tidal Waves briefing. [Confirmed: 2 sources]
