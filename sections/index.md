## Mission type reference

Common objective types across islands: defend a structure/unit, destroy targets (mountains, vats, dams), terraform, protect VIPs, break units out of ice. Per-island objective lists are in each island file. Reputation is earned from mission performance and bonus objectives; perfect clears maximize it (see `mechanics.md` → Scoring and reputation).
