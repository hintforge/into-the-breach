## Island boss

Corporate HQ defense — **Pinnacle is the ONLY island where the Bot Leader can be fought** (an alternative to the Hive Leader). The Bot Leader hunkers down and shields if damaged, and heals to full HP if not killed within the round. Extends mainstream guides, which only say "boss fight." [Confirmed: 2 sources]
_source: deep-research handoff 2026-06-02 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

**Bot Leader scaling by island order:** the Bot Leader's stats vary based on when Pinnacle is attempted. As islands 1–2: base stats. As islands 3–4: +1 HP and +2 damage per tile. It always spawns with 2 additional hostile Sentient Weapons, both starting Frozen. Recommended strategy: save Pinnacle for later in the timeline if the Bot Leader is listed as the boss, so more damage upgrades are available to eliminate it in one turn before it shields. [Confirmed: 2 sources — Fandom Bot Leader (P2)]
_source: deep-research P2 handoff 2026-06-02 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

**Exception:** destroying a mission's shield generator removes ALL active shields including pilot-granted ones (Bethany Jones, Adam, shielding weapons); Mafan's per-turn shield is unaffected. [Single source -- verify · class: wiki]
